Sheets can color cells for you based on a rule, like turning every average under 70 red. When scores change, the colors update on their own.

    Click Conditional formatting

    Set your rule

Color-code grades automatically

Step 3 of 3 · Your turn
You found it!

We can’t choose your cells for you, so here’s how to fill in this panel. Numbers match your screen.

  1. 1
    Apply to range

    Type the cells to color, like E2:E13 for the Average column, or click ▦ and drag over them.

  2. 2
    Format cells if…

    Pick a condition. For struggling students choose Less than, then type your cutoff (like 70).

  3. 3
    Formatting style

    Choose a fill color. A light red works well for “needs help.”

  4. 4
    Click Done

    Your colors appear right away and update as you enter new scores.

Ideas for your class

Flag low gradesLess than → 70 → red
Spot missing workIs empty → yellow
Celebrate top scoresGreater than or equal to → 90 → green
