How each number is measured
Thumbs-up rate (KR 1)
👍 ratings divided by all 👍/👎 ratings on tips, from feedback. Needs at least 5 ratings before it counts. Target 80%.
Tips completed (KR 2)
Walkthroughs followed to their last step (tip_complete) out of those started (tip_open). Target is a completion rate; the headline is the count.
Days the app is opened (KR 3)
For each teacher, the distinct Monday–Friday days this week the side panel was opened (app_open), averaged across teachers. Target 3 of 5 days.
Opens that lead to a tip (KR 4)
Of panel openings, the share where the teacher opened a tip and stayed on it for more than 10 seconds (tip_dwell_success). Target 50%.
Error log
Problems the extension caught itself, plus what teachers report with “Report a problem”, from error_log. “Per 100 opens” counts automatic errors against panel openings in the same window. What teachers type in a report is never shown here.
AI tip builder
Counts of prompts copied and tips built (ai_prompt_copied, ai_tip_pasted, ai_tip_gemini).

All four Key Results read live from Supabase — Objective A from tip ratings and completions, Objective B (days opened, tip engagement) from the shared events table. A card reads “Awaiting data” only until its first events arrive.
