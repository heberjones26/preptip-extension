Product health
How each number is measured
Thumbs-up rate
Thumbs-ups divided by all thumbs-up and thumbs-down ratings on tips. Goal 80%.
Walkthroughs finished
Walkthroughs followed to their last step, out of those started. Goal 60%.
Days a teacher opens PrepTip
For each teacher, the distinct Monday to Friday days in a week they opened the panel, averaged across teachers. Shows the latest week with activity. Goal 3 of 5 days.
Opens that lead to a tip view
Of panel openings, the share where a teacher stayed on a tip for more than 10 seconds. Goal 50%.
Requested tips
What teachers typed into “Submit a tip request”, grouped by site and wording. Links, emails and phone numbers are removed and no names are shown.
Sample data
The Sample switch shows invented numbers so the layout can be seen fully populated. It never touches the database and is always labelled.
